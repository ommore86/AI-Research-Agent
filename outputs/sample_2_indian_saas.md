# Research Report: Find 5 Indian B2B SaaS startups in HR tech and summarize their positioning.

## Executive Summary
The Indian B2B HR tech ecosystem features mature unicorns and fast-growing mid-market leaders. The top 5 prominent players are Darwinbox (enterprise HCM), Keka (SME-first HR and payroll automation), Zimyo (emerging businesses and financial wellness), Hono (AI-driven conversational HRMS), and GreytHR (compliance-centric payroll).

## Key Findings

- **Darwinbox**: Positioned as an enterprise Human Capital Management (HCM) platform competing directly with global giants like Workday and SAP SuccessFactors across APAC and MENA [S1].
- **Keka**: Highly focused on employee experience and automated payroll for mid-market and fast-scaling tech companies [S1, S2].
- **Zimyo**: Tailored for SMBs and startups, offering an integrated suite combining HRMS with employee financial benefits and micro-lending [S1].
- **Hono**: Differentiates through conversational AI chatbots that automate daily employee queries, leave management, and operational workflows [S2].
- **GreytHR**: One of the pioneering cloud HR platforms in India, trusted by over 20,000 businesses for strict statutory compliance and payroll processing [S1].
